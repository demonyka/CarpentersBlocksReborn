# Changelog

## 4.0.0-alpha.1 - 2026-11-10

First public build of the rewrite, for Minecraft 1.16.5 on Forge and Fabric.

- Carpenter's Block (full and slabs), 65 slope shapes, stairs, collapsible block.
- Barrier, gate, door, hatch, garage door, ladder.
- Button, lever, pressure plate, torch and lantern.
- Covers, side covers, dyes and illumination, with the 1.7.10 controls. Side covers take a sneak-click.
- Covers keep the look of the real block: biome colours, rotated textures, translucent glass and ice.
- Connected textures on covers with OptiFine and with Chisel / CTM (Forge).
- Jade / HWYLA tooltip shows the cover (Forge).
- Recipes, drops and cover lists are data-pack driven.

Not included from 1.7.10: safe, bed, flower pot, tile, daylight sensor, chisel patterns, overlays.

This is an alpha: keep backups of worlds you care about.
